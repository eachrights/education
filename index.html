Education Budget Tracker

Kenya's education budget rises 11.6% to KSh 784.5 billion in FY 2026/27. This tracker follows the national and Kilifi County allocations line by line, with the source of every figure.

Budget years FY 2025/26 and 2026/27Coverage National and Kilifi CountyLast updated 2 November 2026

What This Tracker Shows

OXFAM-funded advocacy project

This tracker supports budget advocacy under the project "Taking on the Legal and Policy Advocacy Challenge: Promoting Synergies Among EDUCATION Social Movements in Kenya." It compares National Treasury allocations for FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27, the basic education sub-programmes that reach schools and learners, and the Kilifi County education lines. Teachers' pay takes more than half of the total, while some lines that reach learners directly stay flat or fall.

Where the 2026/27 Education Budget Goes

KSh 784.5 billion, by sub-sector

Source: National Treasury, Budget Statement FY2026/27, paragraph 222 (page 136). The five parts add to KSh 784.6 billion against 784.5 reported, which is rounding.

Where the cuts fall. KPEEL is the largest national line cut, from KSh 13.3 billion to KSh 7.1 billion (−46.6%). In the basic education brief, school health, nutrition and meals falls 38.8%.

Data Notes and Sources

Where sources differ, these notes say which figure is used
  1. The 49% increase quoted by Treasury compares 2026/27 with 2021/22 (KSh 526 billion). Year on year the rise is 11.6%.
  2. FY 2026/27 figures come from the National Treasury Budget Statement of 11 June 2026. FY 2025/26 figures come from the Parliament of Kenya report on the 2025 budget speech, with Capital FM where the report is silent.
  3. TSC 2025/26 is KSh 387.2 billion in the Treasury speech; the committee report gives KSh 387.7 billion. The 2025/26 intern teacher figure (KSh 7.2 billion) sits inside the TSC allocation, while 2026/27 lists KSh 8.2 billion separately, so the two years may not be like for like.
  4. Science, Innovation and Research is KSh 993 million in the 2025/26 speech and KSh 943 million in the committee report.
  5. Kilifi ward scholarships: the draft estimates also show a KSh 400 million development line for 2025/26 that is not included, because it is unclear whether it adds to the KSh 500 million recurrent line.
