These figures do not reconcile to the TSC total. The two sub-programmes add to KSh 399.4 billion in 2025/26, which is more than the KSh 387.2 billion TSC total used above, so the bases probably differ (for example supplementary estimates). They come from a GI-ESCR submission that cites the FY 2026/27 Programme Based Budget [11] and have not been checked against it. In 2026/27, 99.8% of primary and 99.7% of secondary sub-programme spending is staff pay.

Read with care. Releases are exchequer issues against gross estimates, so they run lower than against net estimates (the sector received 77.7% of its KSh 624.7 billion net estimate). Higher Education spent more than it was released because institutions also spend their own income. Spending is from Mzalendo, which reads the Controller of Budget annexes, and has not been checked against them. "—" means not found.
